Pyr has two sets of Achievements you earn just by doing science. One counts the **cells you complete**. The other counts the **edits you make**.

Achievements you earned before the November 2026 update stay yours, in the Legacy Achievements row of your Trophy Case.

Below find a list of the thresholds.

## Cell Completion Achievements

Every cell you complete counts.

- Your **first cell** earns your first Achievement.

- Then **5, 10, 15 and 20** cells.

- Then **30, 40, 50, 75, 100, 125 and 150** cells.

- Every **50** cells up to 500.

- Every **100** cells up to 2,000.

- Every **250** cells up to 5,000.

- Every **500** cells up to 10,000.

- After 10,000, Every **1,000** cells.

## Editor Achievements

Every merge and every split counts as an edit.

- Your **first edit** earns your first Achievement.

- Then **10, 25, 50 and 100** edits.

- Every **100** edits up to 500.

- Then **750 and 1,000** edits.

- Every **500** edits up to 5,000.

- Every **1,000** edits up to 20,000.

- Every **2,500** edits up to 50,000.

- Every **5,000** edits up to 100,000.

- After 100,000, every **25,000** edits.

## What will you unlock next?

Open your Trophy Case to see the ones you hold and how far you are from the next one.
