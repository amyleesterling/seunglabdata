Everything that changed in Pyr this week, newest day first. A new day is added each afternoon, with the Today in Pyr note in your bell.

## Thursday, November 19

Pyr adds a Cilia Quests card to your profile, a right click hold that opens a cell's Δ menu, and a tidier Training Sim box that keeps track of your cell.

**Cilia Quests on profile:** Your profile shows your Cilia Quests today, this week and all time, your leaderboard place, and a bar to your next milestone.

**Δ menu by right click:** Hold the right mouse button still on a cell for half a second to open its Δ menu.

**A tidier Training Sim:** The Training Sim box finds your cell again on its own, folds its hint to one line, and Jump skips spots you fixed.

**Profile cards on laptops:** On laptops, your profile shows Cells completed, Edits and Days helping science as cards, each with a bar to the next Achievement.

**Check marks and fixes:** Finished tutorials get a green check mark, cut and merge bars are blue, and 2D and 3D no longer stay blank when memory runs low.

### All 10 updates

- **Shorter nucleus step.** The Advanced tutorial's nucleus step is shorter.
- **The Training Sim keeps track of your cell.** After a run of merges, the Training Sim box no longer gets stuck on Standby: it finds your cell again on its own. When your Merge is high but nothing is cut yet, the box tells you that cutting off the wrong parts is what will raise your Match score.
- **A smaller Training Sim box, and an easier Jump.** Jump to next spot now has its own full row, and it glows once after a good merge or cut. Highlight folds behind a button next to Save and Skip Cell, and Pyr remembers whether you keep it open. The note under Save fits on one line. Score changes read to the tenth, so a +15 no longer shows as +14.9999. If you make the box smaller, it scrolls, so nothing gets cut off.
- **Your profile on a laptop.** On laptop screens, Cells completed, Edits and Days helping science sit side by side as cards, each with its bar to the next Achievement. Your name comes first, with Special Awards and Cells identified on one line under it. Time on Quests now counts Super Quests inside All MEC Quests, since Super Quests are MEC Quests.
- **A quieter Training Sim box.** The note about cutting off wrong parts has moved from the box into its info button.
- **A clearer cut bar, and the Δ menu on a right click hold.** In cut mode the bar stays blue the whole time, and the side you are placing is the one lit up solid, red or blue. The button beside it now says what G does: Place blue, or Place red. Hold the right mouse button still on a cell for half a second to open its Δ menu, without finding it in the segment list. Your segment list keeps its place when you come back from the Annotations tab, instead of jumping around. When two merge points are too far apart, the game now says so in plain words: click both pieces right where they touch.
- **Check marks on finished tutorials, and a blue merge bar.** In the tutorials menu, a green check mark now follows each tutorial you have finished. The merge bar is blue now, like the cut bar. When your browser tab runs low on memory, Pyr frees some space and reloads the images by itself, so 2D and 3D should no longer stay blank until you refresh.
- **No extra strip under the cut and merge bar.** In cut and merge mode, the extra strip of key names under the bar is gone again, in every browser.
- **Cilia Quests on your profile.** Your profile has a Cilia Quests card with your Quests today, this week and all time. It shows your place on the Quests leaderboard, such as 2nd of 8 this week, and a bar to your next Quest milestone. Quests leaderboard opens the leaderboard right on the Quests tab.
- **A tidier Training Sim box.** The Training Sim hint folds to one line. Click it to read it all and use Show me, and Pyr remembers which way you left it. Merge and Cut in the box look like the rest of the box, and light up blue while their mode is on. Cut mode is blue in the box, like the cut bar. After a page refresh, the box finds your cell again instead of saying it is not on screen. On Standby the box shows only its note and its button. A merge that adds part of your cell now reads Good merge. Jump to next spot no longer sends you back to a spot you just fixed.

*Players made 2,115 edits and completed 87 cells.*

## Wednesday, November 18

The Training Sim gets Merge and Cut buttons, a faster Jump and a Merge score that counts every merge. The Starship Tour can be replayed from the menu, and letter shortcuts work on French and other keyboards.

**Training Sim upgrades:** Turn Merge and Cut on with new buttons, Jump to the next spot faster, and watch every good merge raise your Merge score.

**Replay the Starship Tour:** Pick Starship Tour in the top right menu, just under Site Tour, to fly it again now that the chat box rocket is retired.

**Shortcuts on every keyboard:** Letter shortcuts follow the letter printed on your key, so M merges and C cuts on French and other keyboards.

**A new Play button:** Press the bright teal Play button for a launch flash, rings of light and a comet, then see Teleporting while your cell loads.

**Tutorials and smaller fixes:** Merge and Cut tutorials zoom the 2D images close, the side panel button always opens, and Pyr opens on your cells again.

### All 12 updates

- **Keyboards and the Merge glow.** Letter shortcuts now follow the letter printed on your key, so M merges and C cuts on French and other keyboards too. In the Merge tutorial, the Merge icon on the top bar glows again while the box asks you to turn it on.
- **Clearer Cut tutorial.** The Cut tutorial now says to Ctrl+Click the branch right next to each gem, not the gem itself.
- **The tour rocket lands.** The rocket by the chat box is retired now that the new tour is done. Thank you to everyone who flew it and sent feedback! New players now start the tour only after the sign in box closes, so it never plays hidden behind it.
- **Closer 2D in the tutorials.** In the Merge and Cut tutorials, the 2D images now zoom in close on the spot each step points at. The 2D cut steps remind you that Ctrl and the mouse wheel zoom the 2D images.
- **A new Play button.** The Play button is now a bright teal key with a glass label. Press it and it launches with a flash, rings of light and a comet along the label. While your cell loads, it says Teleporting until you arrive.
- **Fly the Starship Tour again.** Want to see the Starship tour again? Open the menu at the top right and pick Starship Tour, just under Site Tour.
- **Every merge counts in the Training Sim.** Every missing piece you merge in now adds to your Merge score, even a tiny one, so each good merge moves the number. The Match score and Merge show tenths of a percent.
- **Easier to spot pins, clearer mark questions.** In the Training Sim, the pin that shows where to look is bigger, drawn over the cell, and beats like a heart. When you remove a segment that has marks on it, the question now shows that segment in its own color with its number, and Show takes you to the marks before you decide. That question also comes up faster.
- **A faster Jump in the Training Sim.** Jump finds the next spot much faster: the Training Sim looks it up while you work. Jump and Review now take you to a slice with an image, so the 2D view no longer goes black. The Jump pin stays still again, big and drawn over your cell. New Merge and Cut buttons in the Training Sim box turn those modes on and off, just like the M and C keys. A wrong part you cut off now counts as cut off, even when a small stub of it stays on your cell. After a cut, the old copy of your cell no longer stays on screen over the new one.
- **Side panel button always opens.** The side panel button at the top right now opens the cells panel even when no layer is selected. Before, it sometimes did nothing.
- **Opening on your cells again.** Pyr no longer opens on an empty view with no cells. A view with no cells is never kept as where you left off. The Training Sim pin is a little smaller.
- **Smoother Advanced tutorial.** In the Render step, the settings light up one at a time, and each slider moves once on its own to show what it does. Glowing buttons on the top bar now glow all the way around. The nucleus step says what to do if the whole cell comes in instead of just its nucleus.

*Players made 1,896 edits and completed 77 cells.*

## Tuesday, November 17

The Sandbox is now the Training Sim, which scores your cell live against a proofread copy. Cilia Quests get an instructions button, and the Cell Library marks your active cell.

**Meet the Training Sim:** Press Play in the Training Sim and a box scores you against a proofread copy, with Jump to fixes and missed spots shown in gold.

**Quest instructions on hand:** While playing Cilia Quests, tap the small i button on the play bar to open the quest instructions.

**See your active cell:** A green Active tag in the Cell Library shows which cell you are working on, so you know where Save view saves.

**Fixes and polish:** Claim and Skip now pass over cells others hold, finished merges no longer show as failed, and the Highlight box is easier to move.

### All 10 updates

- **Play button setting, claiming fixed.** The Settings switch for the Play button is now called Play button on the 2D view. Turning it off keeps the 2D view clear, and you can still start playing from the Cell Library. Claim a new cell and Skip pass over a cell someone else already holds and claim the next one, instead of stopping with This cell is already claimed.
- **Merges that worked no longer say failed.** When a merge went through but the server was slow to say so, the Merge Queue could still mark it failed. Pyr now checks the cell, and a merge that worked shows as done.
- **Quest instructions, Highlight box.** While playing Cilia Quests, a small i button on the play bar opens the quest instructions. The Highlight box moves out of the way when it would sit under the Cell Library. The slim Highlight strip is easy to move: drag it by the grip at its left end, or by any part that is not a button.
- **Your active cell.** In the Cell Library, the cell you are working on now has a green Active tag by its name, so you always know which cell Save view saves to.
- **A simpler beta request form.** The beta request form now just asks for your email, the best one to reach you.
- **The Training Sim.** The Sandbox is now the Training Sim. Press Play and a box scores your cell as you work, against a proofread copy of the same cell. Merge counts the missing pieces you add back, and Cut counts the wrong parts you cut off. Every cell starts at 0% for you. Jump takes you to the next spot that needs fixing, and Complete and Review shows what you missed in gold. A colored bar on the box shows what a Ctrl+click does: blue for Merge, red for Cut, green for Highlight. Mark where you have been with Checked and Come back, and the 2D view opens beside the 3D when you play. Your Training Sim accuracy shows on your profile and on your Cell complete card. When you skip a sim cell you can say why, and the Pyr team reads it.
- **Training Sim touch ups.** Jump in the Training Sim now always takes you to a spot on your own cell, never out into empty space. Getting your cell ready shows Pyr's glowing neuron while your cell loads. The info boxes in the Training Sim give each term its own heading, so they are easier to read.
- **Sections slider on hover.** In the 3D view, the slider for how solid the sections are now shows only when you hover on Sections, so it stays out of the way.
- **Sections and scale bar restyled.** The Sections switch in the 3D view and the scale bar now match the rest of Pyr: dark rounded chips with a cyan tick box and a thin cyan bar.
- **Training Sim score on your profile.** Your Training Sim accuracy now sits on its own line above Cells completed, so your counts and Achievements stay in their places. Once you can edit the Retina, the Training Sim score no longer shows on your profile.

*Players made 2,092 edits and completed 73 cells.*

## Monday, November 16

Pyr now uses a short page address with a Share button for links, new players get a Starship tour of a real neuron, and Nurro answers in your own language.

**Short links, kept views:** Your view now survives reloads and browser restarts, and the Share button copies a short link that opens your view anywhere.

**A Starship welcome:** New players now begin with a short Starship tour of a real neuron before Get Started.

**Nurro speaks your language:** Nurro answers in the language you write in and knows about folders, Hunts, edit access and every new change to Pyr.

**New keys:** A new layer is now Shift+N, changeable in Settings, Merging, and the Lantern moves to Ctrl+L, or Cmd+L on a Mac.

**Trophy Cases and polish:** Click an Achievement in chat to open that Trophy Case, and enjoy faster cut points, steadier panels, roomier reports and a cilium Start here marker.

### All 8 updates

- **Start at the base of the cilium.** Cilia Quest Training: when you trace the cilium, the view now starts right at its base, where the line begins, with a Start here marker.
- **New keys and steadier panels.** A new layer is now Shift+N instead of N, so it is no longer pressed by mistake next to M. Settings, Merging lets you choose N, Alt+N or no key. The Lantern moves to Ctrl+L (Cmd+L on a Mac). The segment list keeps its place when you switch tabs or layers and come back. The cut bar, the chat and the TODO, PROOFREAD, TYPED, DONE bar no longer jump up for a moment while a cut is sent.
- **A short page address.** The page address is now a short code instead of your whole view, so it no longer weighs down your browser or its history. Your view is kept in your own browser, so a reload, a reopened tab or a browser restart still brings it back. To send a view to someone, use the Share button: it copies a short link that works anywhere. Switching to a dataset picks up where you left off there. To open a dataset fresh instead, pick what a switch opens in Settings > Switching datasets.
- **A Starship welcome.** New players now start with a short Starship tour of a real neuron, then Get Started.
- **Clearer reports, steadier lists.** The report form now opens with room for details, so you can say what happened and what you expected. The segment list now appears right where you left it when you come back to a layer, instead of scrolling down to it.
- **Nurro knows more.** Nurro now answers in the language you write in, and offers your browser's language once. Nurro knows about folders, Hunts, getting your 2D and 3D views back, Log out, asking for edit access and more. Nurro now learns about each change to Pyr as soon as it goes live.
- **Achievements from chat.** Clicking an Achievement someone earned in chat now opens their Trophy Case right at that Achievement.
- **Faster cut points.** Cut points now appear the moment you Ctrl+click, even on very big cells.

*Players made 1,089 edits and completed 57 cells.*
