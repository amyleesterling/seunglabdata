Getting started

All of our datasets are mapped in Neuroglancer / Spelunker, a browser‑based viewer for exploring and editing 3D reconstructions of neurons. The quickest way in: pick a dataset on the Datasets page and sign up, then read the Spelunker guide below to learn the interface before your first proofreading session. When you're ready, the Learn Neuroscience tour shows what you're actually looking at, and the tutorials further down teach the editing tools click by click.
