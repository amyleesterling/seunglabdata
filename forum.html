Citizen Neuroscience

Community forum

This is where citizen scientists ask questions, get unstuck, and report issues. Whatever dataset you are mapping, the community forum is the place to compare notes and get a fast answer. Please note that these datasets and tools are early and in beta, with known issues, so your feedback and bug reports are genuinely welcome.

Beta

Found a bug?

These tools are still being built, so glitches happen. Post the details in the forum so others can help and the team can track it, or email us directly.

  • Report it in the forum Describe what you saw, the dataset, and how to reproduce it.
  • Email the team Prefer email? Send us a note and we'll follow up.
