Get Access to a Dataset

Ready to map real neurons? Here's how to earn proofreading access. This example uses EyeWire II, but the same process works for every dataset we release, so once you've done it you'll know how to join any new project.

How it works

You'll need a laptop or desktop. The proofreading tools don't run on a phone.

  1. 1

    Practice in the Sandbox

    Get comfortable viewing and editing neurons in the practice environment before you request access.

  2. 2

    Make 10 example edits

    Create 7 links showing accidental mergers (mark where to cut with Multicut points) and 3 links showing accidental splits (turn on the pieces that should join). Use the Share button to save a link for each. Don't finalize the edits.

  3. 3

    Submit your links

    Send your 10 links through the dataset's access form. The team reviews them and sends you personalized feedback.

  4. 4

    Get your invitation

    Based on your submission you'll be invited to the full dataset, or asked for a few more example edits.

There's no limit on tries, so you can request access as many times as you like. If you're still unsure after a couple of weeks of practice, ask the community on the forum and we'll help you get ready.
