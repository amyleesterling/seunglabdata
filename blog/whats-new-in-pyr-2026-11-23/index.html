What's new in Pyr: week of November 23

November 23, 2026 · Nurro

Everything that changed in Pyr this week, newest day first. A new day is added each afternoon, with the Today in Pyr note in your bell.

Thursday, November 26#

Cancel now stays available while an edit is sending, so a stuck merge can no longer trap you, and Cilia Quest reopens your cells and views correctly.

Escape a stuck merge: Cancel stays on the cut and merge bar while an edit is sending, so a merge that never answers cannot trap you.

Cilia Quest view fixes: Again opens your submitted view first, your active cell restores its whole view, and a reclaimed cell opens fresh without old marks.

Smaller fixes: Highlight between two points follows the branch to your end point, Training Sim Merged pins sit on the merger, and the Retina form reads clearer.

The update#

  • Cilia Quest fixes, and a way out of a stuck merge. Cilia Quest: Again opens your submitted view first, so your fix, the crosshair check and the new link are all on that cell. Cilia Quest: going back to your active cell brings its whole view back, not just the crosshairs. Cilia Quest: a released cell opens fresh when it is claimed again, without the marks placed before. Cancel stays on the cut and merge bar while an edit is sending, so a merge that never answers can't trap you. Highlight between two points follows the branch and stops at your end point. Training Sim review: each Merged pin sits on the merger itself, not in empty space nearby. The Retina access form's first step reads more clearly.

Players made 2,135 edits and completed 87 cells.

Tuesday, November 24#

Highlight gains a One click choice that colors a branch both ways from a Ctrl+click, Cilia Quest gets a button to swap Basal body and Daughter centriole marks, and the streak card shows all seven flames.

One click Highlight: Ctrl+click a branch to color it on both sides with no start point, and set the reach with the Each way slider.

Swap centriole marks: In Cilia Quest, a new button swaps your Basal body and Daughter centriole marks once you place one, thanks to toknow.

Brighter streak flames: The streak card shows all seven flames in a row, with a gold Kindled flame and a white hot Solar flame.

Lighter on graphics cards: Pyr now keeps up to 1 GB of the cell in graphics memory, so loading, merging and cutting should run smoother.

Fixes and polish: Merger Mysteries are now 12 so no one is stuck, submitted Cilia Quest cells list newest first, and panels display properly again.

All 6 updates#

  • One click highlights and a centriole swap. Highlight has a new One click choice: each Ctrl+click colors the branch on both sides of the click, with no start point. The Each way slider sets how far it reaches. Highlight opens with your highlights shown and Checked in color. Dim lasts for your visit only. Cilia Quest: a new button in the quest bar swaps your Basal body and Daughter centriole marks, for when the two went the wrong way round. Thanks, toknow, for the idea. The quest bar uses the same lettering as the rest of Pyr. An empty annotation layer reminds you to hold Ctrl and click to add a point, and layers added with + Annotation in a Retina cell now record where each point is. The Lost Fragments how-to says Highlight is all you need there, no annotation layers. With cut or merge on, a right click on a cell not drawn in 3D says so in the edit bar instead of pushing the TODO list up. In the Render tab, the color seed box fits again, so the Fixed color swatch is no longer cut off. Bold words in a notification popup now show bold.
  • Lighter on your graphics card. Pyr now keeps up to 1 GB of the cell in graphics memory instead of 2 GB, which should make loading, merging and cutting smoother on computers with smaller graphics cards. If you set your own value in Viewer settings, it stays.
  • Brighter streak flames. The streak card now shows all seven flames in a row, so you can see which ones you have passed and which are ahead. The Kindled flame (7 days) is a bright yellow gold with rising embers, so it no longer looks like a Spark. The Solar flame (365 days) burns white hot, and runs through every flame's color when you open the card.
  • toknow's ideas, and Merger Mysteries unstuck. Thanks to toknow, your submitted Cilia Quest cells now list the newest first. The swap button in the Cilia Quest bar, also toknow's idea, now always shows on a quest and has a fresh look. It works once you place a Basal body or Daughter centriole mark. Merger Mysteries are now 12: one mystery could no longer be opened, and players whose last mystery it was got stuck waiting. It is gone, so they can finish.
  • Swap button tidied. The Cilia Quest swap button shows once you have placed a Basal body or Daughter centriole mark, and stays out of the way until then.
  • Delete button back in view. In an annotation layer's list, each row's Delete button and the annotation count stay inside the side panel, in every browser.

Players made 1,503 edits and completed 58 cells.

Monday, November 23#

A small day of fixes in Pyr: your saved nickname now shows in its box, completing a cell counts once for Achievements, and the community CONGRATS banner plays only once.

The update#

  • Nicknames that show, and fairer counts. The Nickname box in Lost Fragments and the Complete form shows the nickname you saved, instead of opening empty. Completing a cell from the Annotation panel counts it once, so Achievements arrive at the right number. The community CONGRATS! banner plays once, not again each time you come back. Its notification still plays it whenever you like.

Players made 1,128 edits and completed 103 cells.
