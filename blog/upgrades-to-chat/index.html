Upgrades to chat

October 10, 2026 · Amy R. Sterling

Chat is where a lot of Pyr happens: questions about a tricky branch, a link to a cell, a cheer when someone finishes a big one or earns a new achievement. This week it picked up a set of expansions suggested by Krzysztof Kruk. Bonus: None of them takes up any more of your screen.

Bold and underline

There is no toolbar to learn. Put a star, *, on each side of the words you want in bold. Put an underscore on each side of the words you want underlined, like _this_.

Or select some text in the message box and press Ctrl+B for bold or Ctrl+U for underline. Press it again to take the mark off. You can use both on the same words.

The marks only count around a whole word or phrase. A name with an underscore in it, or a sum written with stars, stays exactly as you typed it.

Search the conversation

Click the magnifier at the top of the chat. The line where you type a message becomes a search line, and the chat shows only the messages that match.

  • Type one or more words. A message has to contain all of them.
  • Put a minus sign in front of a word to leave out messages that have it, like merge -queue.
  • Put words in quotes to find them together, like "cut off".
  • Type from: and a name to see one player's messages, like from:Nseraf.
  • Search one day with on:10/8, or a stretch of days with after:yesterday and before:2026-10-01. The words today and yesterday work too.

Click any result to jump to that message in the conversation, with everything around it. Press Esc, or click the magnifier again, to go back to chat.

Search looks through the messages chat has loaded. To reach further back, press Search earlier messages too at the top of the list to include the past 14 days. When you search for a day, chat brings in the earlier messages by itself.

Find your own messages

A thin bar now appears to the left of your own messages, making them easy to spot. If you would rather not see it, open Settings and switch off Mark my own messages in chat.

See what is new

When you open Pyr, or come back to it from another tab, a line across the chat shows where the messages you have not seen begin. It reads New since you left. Everything below it is new. The line goes away once you send a message.

Pinned messages

Sometimes there is one thing everyone should see, like a dataset opening or an event starting. The Pyr team can now pin a message to the top of chat. It sits in one line above the conversation. Click it to jump to the message, or press the small x to hide it for yourself.

Thank you, Krzysztof

Search, bold and underline, and marking your own messages all came from one player's suggestions. If you have an idea for Pyr, send it in with the feedback button. For science!
