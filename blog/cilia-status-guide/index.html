Cilia Status Guide

November 5, 2026 · Celia D

Hello Cilia Hunters!

This is a quick guide to each cilia status type you may encounter. Click each one to see an example. Happy Hunting!

  • Cilium Annotated - Cilium emerges from the cell, both centrioles and cilium centerline are annotated.
  • Cilium Annotated (fixation artifact) - Cilium structure is visible near the surface of the cell, but appears attached/internal to the soma. This is caused by membranes fusing during data collection.
  • Need Help - You can't find any cilium or centrioles, or there's another issue that stops you from annotating the cell. Good to leave clarifying notes in most cases!
  • Need Help: One centriole missing - You found one centriole, but the other is MIA
  • No cilium - Nothing found. This should only be used after review (use Need Help if you are the first annotator)
  • No cilium (BB & DC clear) - No cilium, but centrioles are visible and clearly differentiated (basal body closer to the surface).
  • No cilium (BB & DC unclear) - No cilium, centriole positions make it unclear which is the basal body and which is the daughter
  • Skipped (low quality) - Extensive data artifacting, missing slides, no soma etc, make it too difficult to ID cilium or centrioles
