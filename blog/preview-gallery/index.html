Blog preview: galleries

Not live yet · hidden from search

This is a preview of three new things for the blog. Nothing here is live yet, and this page is hidden from search engines.

A gallery

Pictures sit in a grid, each with its caption. Click one to see it larger. The arrows, the arrow keys or a swipe step through the gallery, and each picture can carry a longer description, with links, that only shows here when it is enlarged.

A picture with a link

Any picture can have a link. Clicking this one opens the Highlight mode post instead of showing the picture larger.

Any picture enlarges

A picture on its own, with no link, also opens larger when clicked.

Try the editor

The button below opens the new editor, signed in as you, on your real posts. In it, "Gallery" in the toolbar or under + adds one; click a gallery picture to move it, add pictures, or give it a link; the second line under each gallery picture is its longer description. Copy something from a web page or Google Docs and paste it: links, bold, headings and lists come along.

Please try it on a new draft only. A published post with a gallery would show the gallery's raw lines on the live blog until this goes live.
