What's new in Pyr: week of November 16

November 16, 2026 · Nurro

Everything that changed in Pyr this week, newest day first. A new day is added each afternoon, with the Today in Pyr note in your bell.

Saturday, November 21#

Lost Fragments now holds the lab's full list of 11,605 untraced pieces, you can tag glia for a Quest, and the whole Pyr community gets milestone celebrations with confetti and fireworks.

Lost Fragments full list: Play through 11,605 untraced retina pieces in Easy, Medium and Hard, starting from the center and passing over pieces others have started.

Community milestone celebrations: When all players together reach a big milestone, everyone sees a banner with confetti, fireworks and Nurro, and Dataset Stats shows the next one.

Tag glia for Quests: Press It's a glia in Lost Fragments and mark its trunk to count it as a Quest and save it for the Glia Quest.

Keep and nickname fragments: Hold up to five Lost Fragments with saved views, set one aside with Later, and give any cell a private nickname.

Resize views, peek underneath: Drag the line between the 2D and 3D views to resize them, and hold D to hide cell colors in 2D.

All 25 updates#

  • Easier to read chat. Chat messages that reply to you or mention you have a fainter highlight, so their words are easier to read. The bright bar on the left still marks them.
  • Faster Cilia Quest list, Asia 2D back. The Cilia Quest list loads much faster. It shows the first 50 open cells and how many more there are, your own cells, and how many other players hold or have finished. Retina 2D images load again for players who chose the copy hosted in Asia.
  • Community milestones. When all Pyr players together reach a big milestone, everyone sees a CONGRATS! banner with confetti, and Nurro flies across once it has landed. The milestones are 5,000 cells completed and every 5,000 after, 100,000 merges and splits and every 100,000 after, 100, 250 and 500 players, and the Lost Fragments milestones. Each one shows once. Close it with the X, and its notification brings it back any time. Dataset Stats shows the next milestone and how many to go.
  • Resize the 2D and 3D views. Drag the line between the 2D and 3D views to give either one more room. Double click the line to split them evenly again. Pyr remembers your split.
  • Lost Fragments: stash a cell, cleaner checklist. If part of a Lost Fragment belongs to another cell, say so: Pyr tells you whether that cell is already proofread, and if not you can proofread it too or stash it for later. Stashed cells show up in a new Stashed group in Lost Fragments, for anyone to proofread. Complete now reads the crosshairs straight away, and the Nucleus step is gone. The cell type list matches the rest of Pyr, and the Lost Fragments instructions have a new look. Some announcements now open full screen. Click the picture to jump straight in.
  • Pinned messages show once. A new pinned message from the team shows at the top until you close it or refresh, and it does not come back after that. Longer pinned messages now show in full, up to six lines.
  • Hold D to see under the colors. Hold D to hide the cell colors in 2D and see the image underneath. Let go and they come back.
  • Community milestone celebrations. A community milestone celebration now plays by itself only if you are on Pyr within a week of it, and only once. If two milestones arrive close together, you see the newest one, with no line of celebrations waiting behind it. Click the milestone in your notifications to play the whole celebration again, confetti and Nurro included. Only the newest milestone stays there.
  • Pinned messages and announcements. A pinned message in chat is now a small chip. Click it to read the whole message, with what players have sent us since Sept 28 and what we built from it. Clicking the Lost Fragments announcement in your notifications opens it full screen again, with its Play button.
  • Fireworks for the Pyr community. Community celebrations now have fireworks, and the confetti bursts out from behind the banner. A test celebration shows everything we have done in Pyr so far, together: cells completed, edits and players. More confetti is coming as we reach new community milestones!
  • Lost Fragments you started stay yours. Once you make a merge or a cut on a Lost Fragment, it stays yours when Play gives you the next one. You can have five started at once. With five, Play takes you back to them until you complete or skip one. Your Lost Fragments now show in My Cells, with Open and Skip.
  • One popup at a time, and a calmer bell. Achievements, celebrations and questions now take turns, so only one popup is on screen at a time. When you open Pyr you get at most one greeting: a new Achievement, a question you need to answer, or the leaderboard. Community milestones always get their turn. Opening Pyr from its plain address brings back where you left off in that dataset, with no bar asking first. Missed three or more days? The daily updates fold into one While you were away note with a link to the week on the blog. News from the team stays unread until you read it, and the red counts stop at 9+.
  • Reports send more easily. Submit an issue now sends when you only fill in the big box: its first sentence becomes the title.
  • Lost Fragments work like claimed cells. Your work on a Lost Fragment saves as you go: the checks, the point, the cell type and your note come back after a reload or on another computer. Pyr follows a fragment to its new ID after merges and cuts, and shows that ID. Start playing is back in Lost Fragments, and it takes you back to the fragment you were working on. My Cells shows each of your fragments by its current ID, with how many steps are done.
  • A Close button under the instructions. The Lost Fragments and Quest instructions now have a Close button at the bottom right, where you finish reading.
  • Pinned messages stay until you close them. A pinned message in chat now stays at the top until you click its ×, instead of going away when you refresh.
  • Lost Fragments save your whole view. Each Lost Fragment you hold keeps its own view, with your annotations, Highlight marks, layers and camera. It saves by itself as you work, and comes back when you open the fragment again. Your Lost Fragments show in My Cells as rows like your claimed cells, with Save view, Release and Complete. The next fragment starts clean, without the last one's marks. Instructions in the Lost Fragments tab now opens the Lost Fragments how to.
  • Smoother slice, steadier cut bar, 2D detail per dataset. The Sections slider in 3D now fades the slice evenly, instead of staying almost invisible until the very end. Short notes while placing cut or merge points, like a supervoxel already being selected, now show where errors do, so the cut bar and the TODO legend stay still. Settings, 2D images: you can choose a different 2D image detail for the dataset you are on, and it switches by itself when you change datasets.
  • Nicknames for your cells. Give a cell your own nickname, like "cell I merged a branch into". It is not a cell type, and only you see it. There is a Nickname box in the Lost Fragments checklist and in the Complete form. It saves as you type, to your account. Your nickname shows instead of the ID in the Segments list, in My Cells and in the side panel, and it stays with the cell after merges and cuts.
  • Later, for Lost Fragments. Later keeps the Lost Fragment you are on in My Cells with your work saved, and gives you a new one. It is in the Before you complete box while you play, and in the full view. You can hold up to 5 at a time. Play never hands you one of your own started fragments as the next one. Play remembers that you were playing Lost Fragments, instead of going back to BC Challenge.
  • 2D detail for every dataset. In Settings, under 2D images, the new Set it for other datasets list lets you choose the image detail for each dataset without switching to it first.
  • No more replayed achievements. An achievement you already earned no longer celebrates again when Pyr opens while you are still signing in.
  • It's a glia, in Lost Fragments. Found a glia in Lost Fragments? Press It's a glia instead of proofreading it. Say whether it is one glia or several cells fused together, then Submit glia. Labelling a glia counts as a Quest, like Cilia Quest, and the glia is saved for splitting later. Each glia is one column rising up, covered in little barb-like projections. The box links to examples of a glia and of glia fused together. Skip in Lost Fragments no longer leaves the 3D view empty.
  • Lost Fragments: the lab's full list. Lost Fragments now has the lab's full list: 11,605 pieces of the retina nobody has traced yet. They come in Easy, Medium and Hard. Easy ones probably have a cell body, and Hard ones are the smallest pieces. Play takes you from the center of the retina outward, and passes over any piece someone else has already started. Some pieces were cut off a cell in the lab's sheets, or may already be one. The fragment tells you which cell, so you can check.
  • Glia get a trunk point, and the Glia Quest has a date. When you tag a glia in Lost Fragments, put the crosshairs in its trunk before you submit. It shows the lab exactly where the glia is. Tagged glia are stashed for the Glia Quest, launching in early December. Researchers from the labs now have gold names in chat. Nurro knows about Lost Fragments and can answer more questions about Pyr.

Players made 1,017 edits and completed 57 cells.

Friday, November 20#

Lost Fragments opens for everyone who edits Retina, your profile now shows Datasets Unlocked emblems, and the Training Sim stops calling good merges wrong.

Lost Fragments are here: Pick an untraced Retina fragment in the Cell Library or Play card, fix it, and it counts as a completed cell.

Datasets Unlocked emblems: Your profile shows an emblem and date for each dataset you unlocked, including CA3, and you can drag them into any order.

Fairer, sharper Training Sim: Pieces the finished cell never placed no longer count as wrong, and the box has a light bulb hint, lightning buttons and remembered widths.

A new Settings layout: Settings is one larger column with a section list, a search box, and a Changed filter showing only what you changed.

More handy additions: Check lab server status in Profile, Datasets, add up to four screenshots to bug reports, and fold away the Cell Library tabs.

All 29 updates#

  • Training Sim stops calling good merges wrong. The Training Sim no longer calls a merge wrong when the finished cell simply never placed that piece. Most of those merges were right. Pieces the finished cell never placed no longer count as wrong parts, in your score, in Show me or in Jump. Some cells showed dozens of wrong parts that were really one. A failed merge left over in your Merge Queue from an earlier visit goes away on its own once those two pieces are already one cell.
  • Clear removes stray merge dots, and a place in line after a reload. Clear in the Merge Queue now also removes leftover red merge dots that stayed on the screen with nothing queued. If you reload partway through a tutorial while every practice cell is in use, you now get a place in line, and your cell loads by itself when it is your turn.
  • Safer names, invites and shared notes. If you use an invite that was sent to a different email, an admin now checks it is you before letting you in. You can no longer change your name to one another player already uses. You can remove only the help requests and tags you made yourself. Anyone can still mark a help request as solved.
  • A shorter cell bar. The cell bar's button now says + Annotation, so the bar stays on one line. A cell you claimed now says My cell.
  • A lighter Training Sim box. Jump to next spot shows a little search running across the button while it looks, and lights up when it finds the spot. The hint folds into a small light bulb beside Jump. Click the bulb to read the hint and use Show me. The Training Sim icon no longer has a box around it. More pieces that the finished cell never checked now count as neither right nor wrong, so fewer good merges are called wrong.
  • Play waits for the tour. New players see the Play button once they have finished the starship tour and closed the tutorials, so it no longer appears in the middle of the tour.
  • Datasets Unlocked on your profile. Your profile now shows the datasets you have unlocked, each with its own emblem, under your favorite Achievement. The buttons at the top of your notifications are bigger and easier to read.
  • A bigger streak flame, and a faster Mine list. Your streak in the top bar is now a bigger flame with the number of days inside it in gold. The flame grows as the number gets longer. The Mine list in the Cell Library no longer freezes the page for a few seconds when you have finished many cells.
  • Datasets Unlocked arrives Nov 20. At 10 am Eastern on Nov 20, your profile shows an emblem for each dataset you have unlocked: the Retina, MEC, FlyWire and BANC. Join us in chat then for the Datasets Unlocked ceremony.
  • A faster Cell Library. The Cell Library does much less work when it opens and updates, so it stays quick even with thousands of cells and many of your own claims.
  • An easier to read streak and a tidier Segments panel. The day count in your streak flame is now dark against the fire, so it is easy to read. On a short screen, the Segments panel no longer shows two scrollbars side by side or cuts off the edge of the list.
  • More screenshots in a report. A bug report can now carry up to four screenshots, so you can show what happened before and after.
  • Steadier merging and scrolling. Clear in Merge Mode now also removes a red dot left by a first point placed outside the data. The bottom bar and the TODO legend no longer jump up while a cut or merge is being sent. With folders, the Segments panel now keeps its place when you switch tabs and come back.
  • A sharper Training Sim box. Play shows a lightning search across the button the moment you click it, and it strikes when your cell is up. Jump to next spot and Complete and Review have clean faces with a small circuit chip around their edges. Jump's lightning runs from pad to pad and stops at the light bulb, which now sits in Jump like a key. The open hint has its words across the top, with Show me on the right under the fold arrow. What you still have to merge in and cut off reads in red. Teleport to a new cell has the same shape as Play, with lightning on click.
  • Training Sim buttons line up. With the light bulb showing, Jump to next spot sits centered over Complete and Review.
  • Nurro flies by. All day today, Nurro flies across the page once an hour to celebrate Datasets Unlocked. Click a player's dataset line in chat to open their profile at their Datasets Unlocked row.
  • Favorites open in the Trophy Case. Clicking a favorite Achievement in your profile, special ones included, now opens the Trophy Case right at it.
  • The Training Sim box powers on. The Training Sim box powers on like a screen when it opens, and powers off when you close it. Keep working and Complete and Review on the finish screen have the same circuit chip edge as Jump. With the light bulb showing, Jump and Complete are balanced so the row looks even. The finish screen buttons stay on one line, even on a small screen.
  • See the 3D shape update, and server status. After a cut or merge, a small note shows while the new 3D shape loads. Your edit is already saved. You can drag the note anywhere. Profile, Datasets now has Server status: whether the lab servers for Retina, the Sandbox and MEC are OK, slow or down. View all on Special Awards in your profile now opens the Trophy Case, like the other rows. Nurro keeps his answers shorter.
  • A new Settings layout. Settings is now one easy column, with a list of sections on the left, a search box, and a Changed filter that shows only what you have changed. The text in Settings is larger. Server status is now one line at the top of Profile, Datasets, and opens by itself if a server is slow or down.
  • A Training Sim box that fits. While the Training Sim checks your edits, lightning runs along the score bar and strikes when your new score is in. The box fits its contents as they change, so it grows when Highlight opens and shrinks back after. Slim and expanded each remember the width you give them. Teleport to a new cell no longer brings the Play button up over the box.
  • Datasets Unlocked, dated. Under each emblem in Datasets Unlocked is the date you unlocked it, or the year for FlyWire and BANC. Everyone who helped proofread CA3, the mouse hippocampus, has a CA3 emblem. Drag the emblems to put them in the order you like.
  • Fold the Cell Library tabs. An arrow at the top of the Cell Library's tabs folds them away, so the header just names the tab you are on. Press All tabs to bring them back.
  • Profile glows and tabs. The glow behind each Achievement on a profile now matches its art: purple for Days helping science, gold for Edits. The profile's tabs show Pyr's own icons instead of emoji, with the neuron trophy for the Trophy Case.
  • Pinned messages and notifications. The pinned message at the top of chat now shows up to three lines, so longer notes from the team can be read in full. The × that closes Notifications now sits in the panel's top right corner and says what it does when you point at it.
  • Quicker Jump, no stuck 3D pill. The Training Sim score bar flashes with one bolt of lightning when your new score is in. Pressing Jump to next spot while your score is still being worked out no longer takes ages: it waits for the score, then finds the spot. Updating the 3D shape no longer sits at 98% when a last piece never arrives. It goes once loading stops.
  • Other players' activity, no 3D note. On another player's profile, the last 14 days chart now shows their activity, not yours. The Updating the 3D shape note after a cut or merge is gone.
  • Lost Fragments are here. Lost Fragments is open to everyone who can edit Retina: pieces of the retina nobody has traced yet, in the Cell Library and on the Play card. Pick one, fix its splits and merges, check every end, and put the crosshairs inside it. Each one counts as a completed cell. Claim a fragment to hold it for you while you work on it. When we complete 500 Lost Fragments together, there is a party. The tab counts down how many to go.
  • Lost Fragments: cell types and instructions. When you complete a Lost Fragment you can now say what kind of cell it is, if you can tell. Lost Fragments has its own instructions. They open the first time you play, and the ? button opens them again. Folding the Cell Library tabs now puts the tab's name in the title, for example Cell Library: Lost Fragments. The Teleport complete card says Your Cell and sits over the middle of the 3D view.

Players made 2,231 edits and completed 86 cells.

Thursday, November 19#

Pyr adds a Cilia Quests card to your profile, a right click hold that opens a cell's Δ menu, and a tidier Training Sim box that keeps track of your cell.

Cilia Quests on profile: Your profile shows your Cilia Quests today, this week and all time, your leaderboard place, and a bar to your next milestone.

Δ menu by right click: Hold the right mouse button still on a cell for half a second to open its Δ menu.

A tidier Training Sim: The Training Sim box finds your cell again on its own, folds its hint to one line, and Jump skips spots you fixed.

Profile cards on laptops: On laptops, your profile shows Cells completed, Edits and Days helping science as cards, each with a bar to the next Achievement.

Check marks and fixes: Finished tutorials get a green check mark, cut and merge bars are blue, and 2D and 3D no longer stay blank when memory runs low.

All 10 updates#

  • Shorter nucleus step. The Advanced tutorial's nucleus step is shorter.
  • The Training Sim keeps track of your cell. After a run of merges, the Training Sim box no longer gets stuck on Standby: it finds your cell again on its own. When your Merge is high but nothing is cut yet, the box tells you that cutting off the wrong parts is what will raise your Match score.
  • A smaller Training Sim box, and an easier Jump. Jump to next spot now has its own full row, and it glows once after a good merge or cut. Highlight folds behind a button next to Save and Skip Cell, and Pyr remembers whether you keep it open. The note under Save fits on one line. Score changes read to the tenth, so a +15 no longer shows as +14.9999. If you make the box smaller, it scrolls, so nothing gets cut off.
  • Your profile on a laptop. On laptop screens, Cells completed, Edits and Days helping science sit side by side as cards, each with its bar to the next Achievement. Your name comes first, with Special Awards and Cells identified on one line under it. Time on Quests now counts Super Quests inside All MEC Quests, since Super Quests are MEC Quests.
  • A quieter Training Sim box. The note about cutting off wrong parts has moved from the box into its info button.
  • A clearer cut bar, and the Δ menu on a right click hold. In cut mode the bar stays blue the whole time, and the side you are placing is the one lit up solid, red or blue. The button beside it now says what G does: Place blue, or Place red. Hold the right mouse button still on a cell for half a second to open its Δ menu, without finding it in the segment list. Your segment list keeps its place when you come back from the Annotations tab, instead of jumping around. When two merge points are too far apart, the game now says so in plain words: click both pieces right where they touch.
  • Check marks on finished tutorials, and a blue merge bar. In the tutorials menu, a green check mark now follows each tutorial you have finished. The merge bar is blue now, like the cut bar. When your browser tab runs low on memory, Pyr frees some space and reloads the images by itself, so 2D and 3D should no longer stay blank until you refresh.
  • No extra strip under the cut and merge bar. In cut and merge mode, the extra strip of key names under the bar is gone again, in every browser.
  • Cilia Quests on your profile. Your profile has a Cilia Quests card with your Quests today, this week and all time. It shows your place on the Quests leaderboard, such as 2nd of 8 this week, and a bar to your next Quest milestone. Quests leaderboard opens the leaderboard right on the Quests tab.
  • A tidier Training Sim box. The Training Sim hint folds to one line. Click it to read it all and use Show me, and Pyr remembers which way you left it. Merge and Cut in the box look like the rest of the box, and light up blue while their mode is on. Cut mode is blue in the box, like the cut bar. After a page refresh, the box finds your cell again instead of saying it is not on screen. On Standby the box shows only its note and its button. A merge that adds part of your cell now reads Good merge. Jump to next spot no longer sends you back to a spot you just fixed.

Players made 2,115 edits and completed 87 cells.

Wednesday, November 18#

The Training Sim gets Merge and Cut buttons, a faster Jump and a Merge score that counts every merge. The Starship Tour can be replayed from the menu, and letter shortcuts work on French and other keyboards.

Training Sim upgrades: Turn Merge and Cut on with new buttons, Jump to the next spot faster, and watch every good merge raise your Merge score.

Replay the Starship Tour: Pick Starship Tour in the top right menu, just under Site Tour, to fly it again now that the chat box rocket is retired.

Shortcuts on every keyboard: Letter shortcuts follow the letter printed on your key, so M merges and C cuts on French and other keyboards.

A new Play button: Press the bright teal Play button for a launch flash, rings of light and a comet, then see Teleporting while your cell loads.

Tutorials and smaller fixes: Merge and Cut tutorials zoom the 2D images close, the side panel button always opens, and Pyr opens on your cells again.

All 12 updates#

  • Keyboards and the Merge glow. Letter shortcuts now follow the letter printed on your key, so M merges and C cuts on French and other keyboards too. In the Merge tutorial, the Merge icon on the top bar glows again while the box asks you to turn it on.
  • Clearer Cut tutorial. The Cut tutorial now says to Ctrl+Click the branch right next to each gem, not the gem itself.
  • The tour rocket lands. The rocket by the chat box is retired now that the new tour is done. Thank you to everyone who flew it and sent feedback! New players now start the tour only after the sign in box closes, so it never plays hidden behind it.
  • Closer 2D in the tutorials. In the Merge and Cut tutorials, the 2D images now zoom in close on the spot each step points at. The 2D cut steps remind you that Ctrl and the mouse wheel zoom the 2D images.
  • A new Play button. The Play button is now a bright teal key with a glass label. Press it and it launches with a flash, rings of light and a comet along the label. While your cell loads, it says Teleporting until you arrive.
  • Fly the Starship Tour again. Want to see the Starship tour again? Open the menu at the top right and pick Starship Tour, just under Site Tour.
  • Every merge counts in the Training Sim. Every missing piece you merge in now adds to your Merge score, even a tiny one, so each good merge moves the number. The Match score and Merge show tenths of a percent.
  • Easier to spot pins, clearer mark questions. In the Training Sim, the pin that shows where to look is bigger, drawn over the cell, and beats like a heart. When you remove a segment that has marks on it, the question now shows that segment in its own color with its number, and Show takes you to the marks before you decide. That question also comes up faster.
  • A faster Jump in the Training Sim. Jump finds the next spot much faster: the Training Sim looks it up while you work. Jump and Review now take you to a slice with an image, so the 2D view no longer goes black. The Jump pin stays still again, big and drawn over your cell. New Merge and Cut buttons in the Training Sim box turn those modes on and off, just like the M and C keys. A wrong part you cut off now counts as cut off, even when a small stub of it stays on your cell. After a cut, the old copy of your cell no longer stays on screen over the new one.
  • Side panel button always opens. The side panel button at the top right now opens the cells panel even when no layer is selected. Before, it sometimes did nothing.
  • Opening on your cells again. Pyr no longer opens on an empty view with no cells. A view with no cells is never kept as where you left off. The Training Sim pin is a little smaller.
  • Smoother Advanced tutorial. In the Render step, the settings light up one at a time, and each slider moves once on its own to show what it does. Glowing buttons on the top bar now glow all the way around. The nucleus step says what to do if the whole cell comes in instead of just its nucleus.

Players made 1,896 edits and completed 77 cells.

Tuesday, November 17#

The Sandbox is now the Training Sim, which scores your cell live against a proofread copy. Cilia Quests get an instructions button, and the Cell Library marks your active cell.

Meet the Training Sim: Press Play in the Training Sim and a box scores you against a proofread copy, with Jump to fixes and missed spots shown in gold.

Quest instructions on hand: While playing Cilia Quests, tap the small i button on the play bar to open the quest instructions.

See your active cell: A green Active tag in the Cell Library shows which cell you are working on, so you know where Save view saves.

Fixes and polish: Claim and Skip now pass over cells others hold, finished merges no longer show as failed, and the Highlight box is easier to move.

All 10 updates#

  • Play button setting, claiming fixed. The Settings switch for the Play button is now called Play button on the 2D view. Turning it off keeps the 2D view clear, and you can still start playing from the Cell Library. Claim a new cell and Skip pass over a cell someone else already holds and claim the next one, instead of stopping with This cell is already claimed.
  • Merges that worked no longer say failed. When a merge went through but the server was slow to say so, the Merge Queue could still mark it failed. Pyr now checks the cell, and a merge that worked shows as done.
  • Quest instructions, Highlight box. While playing Cilia Quests, a small i button on the play bar opens the quest instructions. The Highlight box moves out of the way when it would sit under the Cell Library. The slim Highlight strip is easy to move: drag it by the grip at its left end, or by any part that is not a button.
  • Your active cell. In the Cell Library, the cell you are working on now has a green Active tag by its name, so you always know which cell Save view saves to.
  • A simpler beta request form. The beta request form now just asks for your email, the best one to reach you.
  • The Training Sim. The Sandbox is now the Training Sim. Press Play and a box scores your cell as you work, against a proofread copy of the same cell. Merge counts the missing pieces you add back, and Cut counts the wrong parts you cut off. Every cell starts at 0% for you. Jump takes you to the next spot that needs fixing, and Complete and Review shows what you missed in gold. A colored bar on the box shows what a Ctrl+click does: blue for Merge, red for Cut, green for Highlight. Mark where you have been with Checked and Come back, and the 2D view opens beside the 3D when you play. Your Training Sim accuracy shows on your profile and on your Cell complete card. When you skip a sim cell you can say why, and the Pyr team reads it.
  • Training Sim touch ups. Jump in the Training Sim now always takes you to a spot on your own cell, never out into empty space. Getting your cell ready shows Pyr's glowing neuron while your cell loads. The info boxes in the Training Sim give each term its own heading, so they are easier to read.
  • Sections slider on hover. In the 3D view, the slider for how solid the sections are now shows only when you hover on Sections, so it stays out of the way.
  • Sections and scale bar restyled. The Sections switch in the 3D view and the scale bar now match the rest of Pyr: dark rounded chips with a cyan tick box and a thin cyan bar.
  • Training Sim score on your profile. Your Training Sim accuracy now sits on its own line above Cells completed, so your counts and Achievements stay in their places. Once you can edit the Retina, the Training Sim score no longer shows on your profile.

Players made 2,092 edits and completed 73 cells.

Monday, November 16#

Pyr now uses a short page address with a Share button for links, new players get a Starship tour of a real neuron, and Nurro answers in your own language.

Short links, kept views: Your view now survives reloads and browser restarts, and the Share button copies a short link that opens your view anywhere.

A Starship welcome: New players now begin with a short Starship tour of a real neuron before Get Started.

Nurro speaks your language: Nurro answers in the language you write in and knows about folders, Hunts, edit access and every new change to Pyr.

New keys: A new layer is now Shift+N, changeable in Settings, Merging, and the Lantern moves to Ctrl+L, or Cmd+L on a Mac.

Trophy Cases and polish: Click an Achievement in chat to open that Trophy Case, and enjoy faster cut points, steadier panels, roomier reports and a cilium Start here marker.

All 8 updates#

  • Start at the base of the cilium. Cilia Quest Training: when you trace the cilium, the view now starts right at its base, where the line begins, with a Start here marker.
  • New keys and steadier panels. A new layer is now Shift+N instead of N, so it is no longer pressed by mistake next to M. Settings, Merging lets you choose N, Alt+N or no key. The Lantern moves to Ctrl+L (Cmd+L on a Mac). The segment list keeps its place when you switch tabs or layers and come back. The cut bar, the chat and the TODO, PROOFREAD, TYPED, DONE bar no longer jump up for a moment while a cut is sent.
  • A short page address. The page address is now a short code instead of your whole view, so it no longer weighs down your browser or its history. Your view is kept in your own browser, so a reload, a reopened tab or a browser restart still brings it back. To send a view to someone, use the Share button: it copies a short link that works anywhere. Switching to a dataset picks up where you left off there.
